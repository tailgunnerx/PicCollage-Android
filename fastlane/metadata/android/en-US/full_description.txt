A high-performance, native Android photo collage studio built entirely with Jetpack Compose.

Features:
- 🎨 50 Dynamic Gradient Borders: Neon, Cyberpunk, Holographic, Luxury Metallics, Pastels, Botanicals, and Conical Sweeps.
- 📐 11 Pro Layouts + Custom Grid Builder: Including 3×3 grid and draggable row/column lines.
- 🖼️ Flexible Aspect Ratios: Square, Portrait, Landscape, Story (9:16), Wide (16:9), or Custom W:H.
- 🔲 Customizable Photo Corners & Borders: Precision sliders for border width and square-to-rounded corners.
- 🔄 Slot-Based Photo Management: Pinch-to-zoom, pan, 90° rotation, and instant photo removal.
- 🌈 Advanced Color Control: Full-spectrum HSV picker and vibrant solid palettes.
- 💾 High-Resolution Export: Standard (1080p), High (1440p), and Ultra (2160p) crisp JPEG output saved to your Gallery.

Optimized for speed and aesthetic control with zero external library overhead. 100% Offline and Private.
